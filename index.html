^1.2.3 means >=1.2.3 <2.0.0: 1.9.9 gets in, 2.0.0 does not, and 1.3.0-beta.1 is skipped because pre-releases need to be named.

What it does

  • Caret, tilde, x-ranges, hyphen ranges, comparators and || alternatives
  • Per-version verdict with the reason, including the pre-release rule
  • Sorts by semver precedence and suggests patch, minor and major bumps
  • Checked against 1,313 results from npm semver 7.8.5
npm range semantics; Cargo, pip and Maven differ. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
